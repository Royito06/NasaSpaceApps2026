## AI assistance

The team used **Claude** (Anthropic, https://claude.ai) and **Gemini** (Google, https://gemini.google.com) to help with code, data analysis and writing. **No AI-generated images, music or art** are used: all visuals are the human-made assets above, NASA imagery, or screenshots of the game.
