# Judging and submission rules (official Judging & Awards Guide, saved 13 Nov 2026)

Source: spaceappschallenge.org/resources/judging-awards-guide/ (PDF in the user's Downloads, read 2026-11-14).

## Hard requirements to be eligible for Global Judging

- **Submit before Sunday 15 Nov 2026, 11:59 p.m. Hermosillo time:** Project tab → edit → "submit".
- Answer one official challenge (ours: Build a Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer).
- All members (max 6) are registered for the **same Local Event** and appear on the team's Members tab. No adding members after submission closes.
- Complete every required field on the Project Submission page.
- **Demo: EITHER a slide deck of at most 7 slides (title and intro count) OR a video of at most 30 seconds.**
- **English on the submission page and in ANY linked materials** (repo, docs, game). A demo video may be spoken in another language **only with English subtitles**.
- Tick both boxes: the submission guide plus T&Cs, and "original work; every external resource (code, text, images, even open source) is listed in the References field; no copyrighted material without permission".
- **China funding restriction:** NASA reviews teams for prohibited bilateral activity with China or Chinese-owned entities (government, companies, universities). Eligibility is at NASA's sole discretion.

## Scoring: 1-5 on each criterion, at every level

- **Impact:** size of the problem and reach. A 5 means significant problem, large audience, effectiveness far beyond expectations.
- **Creativity:** novel, or a meaningful improvement. A 5 means groundbreaking and never attempted.
- **Validity:** scientifically sound, technically feasible, **usable and user-friendly**. A 5 means fully developed and ready for real use.
- **Relevance:** directly addresses the challenge; **NASA data is a core component, not a superficial addition**.
- **Presentation:** clear challenge, solution and significance, with engaging storytelling. **Judges score the demo AND the full project.** The guide stresses giving this "equal attention".

## Timeline

Local judges pick Global Nominees (announced **3 Dec 2026**), then Global Finalists and Honorable Mentions (**17 Dec**), then Global Winners (**26 Jan 2027**).
